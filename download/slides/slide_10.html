Section 03 · Feasibility, Architecture & Risk

Architecture: conservative by design — and right

check_circle

What is strong

  • ✓Explicit state machine instead of a heavyweight agent framework — no hidden non-determinism in a system whose thesis is determinism
  • ✓Evidence is a first-class object: 22-entity model, event-style requirement versioning (never overwrite; author, source, reason, timestamp stored)
  • ✓Authority-aware retrieval: approved policies (authority 1.00 / 0.95) cannot be silently overridden by AI inference (0.3×)
  • ✓Eight hallucination controls wrapped around the human approval gate.

What is unspecified

✕ Context management, concurrency, and observability are not yet designed → run graph-query and infrastructure spikes before Phase 3 (Action 5).
