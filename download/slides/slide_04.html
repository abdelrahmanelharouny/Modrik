What is RequirementPilot?

Rather than generating polished requirements from a single prompt, RequirementPilot conducts structured stakeholder interviews, distinguishes explain-why questions from extraction tasks, and grounds every requirement in versioned evidence. Humans approve each transition — no requirement gap is ever silently filled by a model.
